Fonts
=====

betaflight_default.mcm
betaflight_large.mcm
betaflight_bold.mcm
  Betaflight's default, large and bold OSD fonts, from Betaflight
  Configurator (https://github.com/betaflight/betaflight-configurator,
  resources/osd/2/default.mcm, large.mcm and bold.mcm). Copyright the
  Betaflight contributors, licensed under the GNU General Public License
  version 3 - see GPL-3.0.txt in the fpv_osd folder. Included unchanged; each .mcm file is its own
  source. BrainFPV's firmware carries the same three.

brainfpv_8x8.png
brainfpv_8x10.png
  BrainFPV's two small fonts - the outlined 8x8 one its pitch ladder and
  scales are numbered in, and the 8x10 one in a scale's value box - from its
  Betaflight fork (https://github.com/BrainFPV/betaflight,
  src/main/brainfpv/fonts_stm32h7xx.c, data_font_outlined8x8 and
  data_font8x10), drawn out as 16 x 16 grids of characters by character
  code: clear, black, grey or white. Copyright the BrainFPV and Betaflight
  contributors, licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 - see
  GPL-3.0.txt in the fpv_osd folder.

Any Betaflight .mcm font works: put it in this folder and set font= in
settings.cfg to its file name.
