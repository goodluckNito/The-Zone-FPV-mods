WHY GPL
  Parts of osd.gd are translations into GDScript of code from two GPL
  firmware projects:

  Betaflight (https://github.com/betaflight/betaflight), GPL-3.0-or-later,
  copyright the Betaflight contributors:
    the battery voltage filter and warning state machine (sensors/battery.c),
    the warnings and their order (osd/osd_warnings.c), the stats screen
    and distance formatting (osd/osd.c), the artificial horizon, horizon
    sidebars, crosshair and stick overlay elements (osd/osd_elements.c).

  BrainFPV's Betaflight fork (https://github.com/BrainFPV/betaflight),
  GPL-3.0-or-later, copyright BrainFPV and the Betaflight and Tau Labs
  contributors:
    the graphical stick overlay, pitch ladder and altitude and speed scales
    (src/main/brainfpv/osd_utils.c and brainfpv_osd.c), its crosshair
    (osdBackgroundCrosshairs_BrainFPV in osd/osd_elements.c) and its two
    small fonts (fonts/brainfpv_8x8.png, fonts/brainfpv_8x10.png).

  The .mcm fonts in fonts/ are Betaflight's, unchanged - see fonts/FONTS.txt.

  The source is the mod itself: every .gd, .gdshader and .cfg file here is
  plain text.
